FAQ Section

FAQs

What printing method do you use? +
We use high-quality direct-to-garment (DTG) printing to ensure vibrant colors and long-lasting designs.
Are the prints fade-resistant? +
Yes, our prints are designed to last with proper care. We recommend washing in cold water and air drying for longevity.
How long does it take to process and ship an order? +
Since we operate on a print-on-demand model, orders typically take 2-5 business days for production and 5-10 days for shipping, depending on your location.
Can I track my order? +
Absolutely! Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email.
Do you accept returns or exchanges? +
Since our products are made to order, we only accept returns or exchanges for defective or incorrect items. If you receive a damaged product, contact us within 7 days of delivery.
